# Independent bounded review — Ages and Dispensations

Reviewer: the separate Larkin “Rightly Dividing” data agent. This review inspected the selected original chart image and scanned book pages, independently of the Ages data author. It is deliberately limited to the high-consequence boundaries requested by the project owner.

## Evidence inspected

- `dist/assets/larkin-ages-original.gif`: original plate, “Ages and Dispensations.”
- `research/larkin-book-page-39.png`: PDF page 39 / printed page 24, Legal and Ecclesiastical definitions.
- `research/larkin-book-page-40.png`: PDF page 40 / printed page 25, Church and judgment parenthesis; Age of Ages opening.
- `research/larkin-book-page-43.png`: PDF page 43 / printed page 28, Perfect Age and Omega ages.
- `research/larkin-ages.json`: generated modern data.

Canonical linked book: https://www.earnestlycontendingforthefaith.com/Books/Clarence%20Larkin/RightlyDividingTheWord/LarkinRightlyDividingTheWord.pdf

## Findings

| Check                        | Result                       | Evidence and reason                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Eight numbered dispensations | PASS                         | Plate visibly prints 1–8, with 8 above Fulness of Times. The Tribulation remains an unnumbered interval. Data preserves both facts.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Legal endpoint               | PASS                         | Printed page 24 expressly says Exodus to Birth of Christ. Data preserves that endpoint and separately identifies the plate’s less specific Moses-to-Christ wording and 1491-year label.                                                                                                                           |
| Two Ecclesiastical bounds    | PASS                         | Printed page 24 expressly gives Cross-to-Crown for Christ and Pentecost-to-Rapture for the believer. Both appear in data, with the plate’s Christ-to-Antichrist label kept as a separate distinction.                                                                                                             |
| Times of the Gentiles start  | PASS                         | Original horizontal line starts at B.C.606 within the Legal circle. Data’s startLabel and summary retain that historical label and within-period placement. It is not moved to Moses.                                                                                                                             |
| Times of the Gentiles end    | PASS with display refinement | The line ends at the Revelation / Olivet boundary after the Tribulation. Data summary states that correctly, but its relationship needs `endLabel: “The Revelation”` so the rendered To field does not fall back to the containing period title “The Tribulation.” This small change was sent to the data author. |
| Perfect Age versus Omega     | PASS                         | Plate places Perfect Age / dispensation 8 inside Age of Ages and the Time envelope; Omega is outside. Printed page 28 associates the transition with surrender of the Perfect Kingdom to the Father. Data preserves this distinction.                                                                             |

## Additional bounded observation

The Priesthood relationship summary correctly identifies Ascension to the Judgment Seat of Christ from the plate’s upper bracket. Supplying those same exact values as `startLabel` and `endLabel` would make the relationship’s From/To fields as precise as its summary. This is a display-data refinement, not a contrary doctrinal claim.

No broader transcription or exhaustive citation audit is claimed by this bounded review. The source data author retains the full source-verification report, while the original plate preserves fine graphical detail.
