# Independent content review — Larkin, Rightly Dividing the Word of Truth

Reviewed 2026-11-08 by the independent Larkin-Ages researcher. Scope: `research/larkin-dividing.json`, the exact original GIF supplied in the app, the downloaded chapter-V source and its normalized text, and the Yale MAVCOR object record identifying the 1920 enlarged edition.

## Result

**PASS following the bounded corrections below.** The original data author made the changes; this independent reviewer did not edit the data file.

The review is of source fidelity. It does not certify Larkin’s interpretive scheme or printed chronology as independently established history, science or fulfilled prophecy.

## Requested completeness additions

These are **missing from the modern data at the first review**, not absent from the source-image view:

1. **Resurrection of Christ / First Fruits.** The original vertical branch at the First Advent explicitly labels both. Add a distinct event or a clearly named subevent. Chapter V independently explains the First Fruits typology and cites **1 Corinthians 15:20**. This must not be collapsed into the later resurrection at the Rapture.
2. **Tribulation Saints / Gleanings.** At the Revelation the chart has an additional rising branch with these two labels. Add this event or retain it explicitly in the Revelation details, separate from the Rapture’s Harvest. No verse should be invented merely to fill the reference array.
3. **Elijah.** The original Legal-dispensation circle shows Elijah’s upward branch. Since Enoch is already indexed as a modern event, add Elijah as an internal Legal-period event for equivalent treatment. The main drawing prints no verse beside this label.

The author was notified and asked to retain exact source labels. `duringPeriod` / `withinPeriod` markers must be used consistently in rendering; an internal event must not be turned into a new chronological boundary.

## Additional source-variation note requested

The opening of chapter V, **§IV, The Age of Ages**, says that the Present Age ends with the **Rapture**, followed by the Great Tribulation, and that the Age of Ages starts at the Revelation. In contrast, **§III, The Present Age**, and the drawn Present-Age arc extend that age to the **Revelation**. The data’s modern age span correctly follows the drawing, but this internal prose variation should be noted alongside the existing Antediluvian-Age and Legal-boundary differences. Do not silently rewrite one passage to match another.

## Exact reference-range request

The first-reviewed Messianic entry uses **Revelation 20:1–9**. Its declared source paragraph separately cites **20:1–3** and **20:7–9**, which do not include verses 4–6. Requested the two exact source ranges instead of silently enlarging the author’s citation. This is a citation-fidelity correction, not a claim that verses 4–6 are irrelevant to the subject.
