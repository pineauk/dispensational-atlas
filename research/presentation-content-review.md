## 3. Boundaries and relationships a slide must preserve

- **Scofield:** Promise ends in Egyptian bondage. Law’s closing judgment is the dispersions, with narrative continuing to the crucifixion. Grace begins at Christ’s sacrificial death, and the catching up is the first event in its closing. The chapter gives no fixed seven-year tribulation duration.
- **Ages:** Legal ends at Christ’s **birth** in the chapter, while the plate says Moses to Christ. Ecclesiastical is **Cross to Crown for Christ** and **Pentecost to Rapture for the believer**. Do not replace these with Scofield’s bounds.
- **Rightly Dividing:** retain the Legal endpoint’s textual variation; Ecclesiastical begins at the descent of the Holy Spirit and closes at the Rapture. Keep the Second Advent’s Rapture and Revelation stages separate.
- **Both Larkin studies:** Postdiluvian ends at Abraham; Babel lies within it. Fulness of Times follows the Millennium and the transition by fire/final judgment, and precedes the outer Omega field.
- `events.afterPeriod` is a navigation anchor, not always an exact boundary coordinate. Enoch, Babel, Elijah, heavenly judgment and marriage imagery, and the judgment of the Jews occur within their described periods or interval. Any displayed event placement must also read its `summary`.
- Keep the Judgment Seat of Christ distinct from the Great White Throne. If _Rightly Dividing_ shows its five judgments, retain the original separate numbers and subjects: believers for sin, believers for works, the Jews, the nations, the wicked.
- If resurrection branches appear, retain **First Fruits**, **Harvest**, **Gleanings** (_Rightly Dividing_), and **Tares** at their distinct events. Do not add the Gleanings branch to the _Ages_ drawing by borrowing it from the other chart.
- If relationship bands appear, Times of the Gentiles begins **within Legal at B.C. 606** and ends at **the Revelation**; it differs from Fulness of the Gentiles. The two Paradise labels and underworld branches in _Rightly Dividing_ are spatial relationships, not extra timeline periods.

## 4. Minimum presentation framing

Each overview should identify its author and exact study, state that the modern layout is **schematic and not to scale**, and retain a source citation or route to its source view. Historical dates and durations must remain attributed source labels. Do not infer present-day dates from spacing. The original scan should remain available for details omitted from the overview.

Use exact KJV text for newly displayed Bible quotations from the verified reader. Original author excerpts remain author excerpts: Scofield’s John 5:24 and Ephesians 2:8–9 quotations explicitly use **R.V.**, and some Larkin source quotations likewise carry R.V. labels. Preserve those labels if such excerpts are shown. Presentation polish must not turn historical commentary into a quotation attributed to the KJV.

**Review result:** the existing datasets support accurate simplified overviews without changing source content, provided these numbering, hierarchy, boundary, and attribution distinctions survive the presentation layout.
